Your first visit

What to expect.

First visits take about 45 minutes. Wear something comfortable you can move in.

  1. Arrive five minutes earlyFill in a short form on your phone, or at the front desk.
  2. A chat about what's going onWhen it started, what makes it better or worse, and what you want to get back to.
  3. An assessmentYour physio looks at how you move and checks the area that's bothering you.
  4. A plan, togetherYou'll leave knowing what's likely going on, what the next few weeks look like, and exercises to start with.

Do I need a referral from my GP?

Not for a private appointment. If your GP has given you a Chronic Disease Management plan, bring it along and you may be able to claim a Medicare rebate.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable you can move in. Shorts are handy for knee and hip problems.

Can I bring my scans?

Yes, please do. Bring any X-ray, MRI or ultrasound reports you have.
