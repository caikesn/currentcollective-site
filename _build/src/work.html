The job

Synergy leases fitted consulting rooms and offices to allied health practitioners and small businesses. Starting your own clinic can mean a fit-out costing tens of thousands before you see a single client, so the site's job is simple: show what's available, make the cost obvious, and get people booking an inspection.

What I built
  • Room cards with photos, sizes and an "Ask" button. Tap a few rooms and they're all added to one enquiry
  • A cost comparison against fitting out your own clinic
  • A second band for the other location, in that building's own colours, with its own photo viewer
  • FAQs on subletting and combining offices for a bigger discount
  • An inspection button you can reach from anywhere on the page
  • The enquiry form, privacy policy and domain set up, and the site live on fast hosting

The job

TruAssist Tribe is a family-owned disability support provider working across Moreton Bay and the Whitsundays. They wanted off Squarespace and onto WordPress, keeping the look they already had, and they needed somewhere for their staff and independent support workers to do training online: TruTayff Academy.

What the audit found first
  • 7 of 12 pages had no description for Google
  • Most pages had no main heading, so Google couldn't tell what they were about
  • Old page links led to "page not found", with no redirects
  • The training "Register" button went to an error page
  • Events only existed as a monthly image, unreadable on a phone or to Google
  • An empty "Store" page was still live and listed for Google
What I built
  • All 11 pages rebuilt in WordPress in their own green and navy, with a proper heading and Google description on every page
  • Photos resized and converted, so pages load fast
  • A clear "join the Tribe" enquiry form that asks the right questions up front
  • A "work with us" form with resume upload, for recruiting support workers
  • Events as real tables that read on a phone
  • A team page with all 16 staff and drop-down bios
TruTayff Academy (in progress)
  • An online course platform for staff and independent support workers
  • Course pages, sign-up, payment, lessons, quizzes and certificates in one flow
  • The dead Register button replaced with a real sign-up
