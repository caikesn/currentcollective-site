[ 03 ] What I check

Five things. The ones that cost bookings.

[ 01 ]Speed on a phoneGoogle PageSpeed

How long your page takes to show anything on a normal phone connection, and what's slowing it down. Usually it's oversized photos or a slider.

[ 02 ]Getting enquiriesButtons & forms

Is it obvious how to book, call or ask for a quote? Does the form actually reach you? Can people tap your number?

[ 03 ]Showing up on GoogleSearch basics

Page titles, the description under your Google result, your suburb and services in the right places, and the link to your Google Business Profile.

[ 04 ]TrustFirst impression

Real photos, who you are, what you do and where, opening hours, and whether it looks current. For clinics, I also flag anything that might clash with AHPRA's advertising rules.

[ 05 ]TrackingWhere leads come from

Whether you can see which enquiries came from Google, Facebook or word of mouth, and whether any old tracking code is cluttering the site.
