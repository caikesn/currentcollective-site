[ 03 ] FAQ

Fair questions

Anything else, just ask.

Do I need to pay for anything up front?

Not for the audit or the mockup call. If you go ahead, it's 50% to start and 50% when the site is live.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Once it's paid for, the site and the domain are in your name. If you ever leave, you take all of it with you.

What's not included in the price?

Your domain name (usually about $20 to $40 a year), paid plugins or booking software you already use, and stock photos if you need them. I'll list anything like that in the quote.

Can I make changes myself?

Yes. Or leave it to me: the care plan includes an hour of changes every month.

I'm a health practitioner. Is this AHPRA-safe?

Every page follows AHPRA's advertising rules: no clinical testimonials, no "best in town" claims and no promised results. That's general information, not legal advice, so check AHPRA's advertising hub if you're unsure.

Do you do social media or ads?

Not right now. I'm focused on websites and lead systems, because that's where I can make the biggest difference.
