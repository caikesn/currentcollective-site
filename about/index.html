[ 01 ] Who's building it

Hi, I'm Connor.

Current Collective is just me. I live in Moreton Bay, and I design and code every site myself. You're not dealing with an account manager or getting passed around. If you message, you get me.

I got into this because I like figuring out how things work, then making them work better. A slow site, a form that never reaches anyone, a phone number you can't tap: each one is a small fix that quietly costs a business bookings.

I'll be honest, I'm new. The two businesses on the work page are what I've done so far, and both owners have offered to refer me to people they know. That's why the audit is free, so you can judge the work before you spend anything.

For clinics, every page I build follows AHPRA's advertising rules. No clinical testimonials, no "best in town" claims and no promising results.
