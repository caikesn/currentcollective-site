[ About ] Profile

One person. Start to finish.

Current Collective is Connor Aikens, a web designer and developer in Moreton Bay. He designs and codes every site himself, so if you message, you get him.

I got into this because I like figuring out how things work, then making them work better. A slow site, a form that never reaches anyone, a phone number you can't tap: each one is a small fix that quietly costs a business bookings.

You're not dealing with an account manager or getting passed around. One person quotes it, designs it, builds it and looks after it.

"A site that loads in two seconds beats a beautiful one nobody waits for."

I'll be honest: I'm new. The two businesses on the work page are what I've done so far. That's why the audit is free, so you can judge the work before you spend anything.

For clinics, every page follows AHPRA's advertising rules. No clinical testimonials, no "best in town" claims and no promising results.
