[ 08 ] FAQ

Fair questions

More on the services page.

Is the audit actually free?

Yep. No card and no contract. You get the list of fixes either way, and you're welcome to do them yourself.

How long does a build take?

Mostly it depends on how fast I get your logo, photos and a few notes about the business. Once I have those, you get a launch date with the quote.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Once it's paid for, the site and the domain are in your name. If you ever leave, you take all of it with you.

I'm a health practitioner. Is this AHPRA-safe?

Every page follows AHPRA's advertising rules: no clinical testimonials, no "best in town" claims and no promised results. That's general information, not legal advice, so check AHPRA's advertising hub if you're unsure.

I'm a tradie and barely look at emails. Does that matter?

No. Plenty of owners would rather text, so that's how I'll check in if it suits you. The lead system sends enquiries to your phone as well.
