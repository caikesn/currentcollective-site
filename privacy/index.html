[ Privacy ]

Your details, kept simple.

This is a plain-English summary of what happens to the details you send through this site. Last updated October 2026.

What I collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, business, phone number, email, website and message. The forms also note which page you arrived on and whether you came from Google, Facebook or another site, so I can see which ways of finding me actually work.

What I use it for

To reply to you, do the audit or mockup you asked for, and follow up once. I don't sell or share your details, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

Where it's kept

Form messages are handled by Netlify, which hosts this site, and land in my email. If you become a client, your details are kept with your project records.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. It remembers how you arrived for the length of your visit only.

Getting your details removed

Email con.aikens@gmail.com and I'll delete them.
